# Liquid Glass after the iOS 26 launch: iOS 26.x point releases and the 27-generation OSes

Research date: 2026-11-02. All text is paraphrased. Confidence labels:
- **confirmed**: in Apple's own material (newsroom, apple.com/os, support.apple.com release notes, developer.apple.com docs or session transcripts) or in shipped software reported by several reputable outlets.
- **beta**: seen in developer or public betas, or a single reviewer's hands-on report.
- **rumor**: reporting from before the announcement (for example Gurman/Bloomberg).

## Answers

### 1. Visual and behavioral changes
- **26.x:** Clear/Tinted setting (26.1). Lock Screen clock glass slider and Solid option, plus springier menu animations (26.2). Reduce Bright Effects and a stronger Reduce Motion for glass (26.4). Apple's support page lists nothing else in 26.3, 26.5 or 26.6. The 26.7 notes repeat the 26.4 items word for word, which may be an editing error.
- **27:** The material itself was retuned (stronger diffusion of the background, darkened rim, brighter specular highlights, more uniform refraction, higher contrast, lower default transparency). A continuous system slider replaces Clear/Tinted. Bars now show a uniform scroll-edge toolbar zone. App icons are sharper and more layered, with selective refraction. On iPad and Mac: edge-to-edge sidebars with colored icons, one window corner radius on macOS, clearer active-window distinction, and menu icons hidden by default. watchOS 27 gets the material refinements. visionOS 27 has no Liquid Glass-specific changes that I could find.
- **Tab bar:** I found no Apple-documented tab bar changes beyond the general material and slider changes. Claims of a tab bar "fix" come only from social media and low-quality blogs, so they are unverified.

### 2. Developer APIs and deprecations
- **Confirmed:** Existing glass apps inherit the refinements without recompiling. AppKit adds an interactive glass bounce, `cornerConfiguration` / `.containerConcentric` concentricity, new `NSScrollEdgeEffectStyle` behavior and "show borders" on macOS. A new menu-icon API exists on macOS and iPadOS (in SwiftUI, reportedly via `.labelStyle(.titleAndIcon)`). Icon Composer gains refraction annotations and a preview for earlier OS releases.
- **Opt-out ends:** `UIDesignRequiresCompatibility` is ignored when building with the 27 SDKs (documentation, State of the Union and DTS all agree). Builds with the iOS 26 SDK still honor it. Because uploads must use the 27 SDKs from April 2027, the opt-out is effectively over at that point.
- **Not found:** A brand-new SwiftUI glass modifier or new `Glass` variants in iOS 27. Apple's SwiftUI session page would not load, so this is unverified. Treat the SwiftUI details as secondary-source only.

### 3. Name, design kits and guidance
- "Liquid Glass" is still the official name on apple.com/os/ios, in the iOS 27 release notes, in Settings ("Liquid Glass" slider) and in WWDC26 sessions.
- Figma and Sketch UI kits exist for iOS/iPadOS 27 and macOS 27, plus a 27 App Icon Template. No UI kit is listed for watchOS 27 or visionOS 27.
- The WWDC26 Design guide describes refinements for consistency, readability and accessibility. I could not fetch the HIG "What's new" page, so I can't list its specific HIG changes.

### 4. Criticism and accessibility responses
- **Criticism:** In 2025, legibility and contrast over busy backgrounds, flashing and bright highlights, motion, icons that looked blurry at small sizes, and on macOS Tahoe, floating sidebars, inconsistent window corners and menu icons. Commentary in January 2026 (Macworld, TidBITS) also criticized Tahoe's menu icons.
- **Apple's responses, in order:** Tinted option (26.1, citing beta feedback). Lock Screen clock control (26.2). Reduce Bright Effects and stronger Reduce Motion (26.4). In 27: the retuned material framed explicitly as a readability fix, the user slider, glass that respects Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast, sharper icons, the uniform scroll-edge toolbar, edge-to-edge sidebars and consistent corners.
- **Remaining issues:** Beta testers reported the slider's clear end and the bottom Home Screen search bar as still hard to read.

## Gaps and caveats
- I found no Apple newsroom press release dedicated to iOS 27. Apple's WWDC26 umbrella release plus the apple.com/os pages are the primary sources.
- I could not load the developer.apple.com session pages for SwiftUI (269), the HIG "What's new" page, or the Icon Composer session (251). The SwiftUI API list relies on a dev.to recap.
- Slider details differ between platforms: MacRumors and Six Colors describe the iOS slider as continuous, while 9to5Mac describes three detents on macOS.
- Exact release dates for macOS 27, watchOS 27 and iOS 26.4 were not stated in the sources I fetched (macOS 27 was out by 2026-09-22).
